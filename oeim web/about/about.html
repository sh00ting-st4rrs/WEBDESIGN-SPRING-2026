ABOUT

Nature is everywhere and not enough people realize how much is out there. Of Earth In Mind is here to fix that!

As an ecological education brand rooted in Virginia, our mission is to help everyday people identify plants, understand the importance of native status, and learn how to interact with the land in healthy ways! Whether you're foraging, gardening, or just curious about what's growing in your backyard, we're here to guide you — with a quiet nod to the deeper connection that comes from paying attention. Because when you know the land, you're more likely to care for it!
